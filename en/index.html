For foreign entrepreneurs

Running a transport business in the Czech Republic as a foreigner

We work with many entrepreneurs from abroad. Please read this first, so you know what is and is not possible.

The licence is Czech

The Czech road haulage licence (koncese) and the EU Community licence are issued only to a business established in the Czech Republic: a Czech limited company (s.r.o.), a branch of a foreign company registered in the Czech Commercial Register, or a Czech sole trader.

What your Czech business needs

  • Registered office or premises in the Czech Republic
  • At least one vehicle registered in the Czech Republic
  • Financial standing (EUR 9,000 for the first truck, or insurance)
  • Good repute and a transport manager, which we provide

What we cannot do

We cannot lend or rent a Czech licence to a company registered in another country, and we do not act as transport manager for companies registered abroad. A transport manager must really supervise the company's transport, so we only work with businesses that genuinely operate from the Czech Republic.

Language

You can contact us in English. Applications, contracts and decisions of Czech authorities are in Czech; we will explain what each document means and what you need to do.

Everything you need to know

A transport manager (in Czech odpovědný zástupce, often called garant) is a person with a certificate of professional competence. The law requires one for road haulage over 3.5 t. Without a registered transport manager you cannot get the Czech road haulage licence (koncese) or the EU Community licence. The transport manager does not have to be your employee; an external one can be appointed by contract.

The Community licence (in Czech Eurolicence) allows your Czech company to carry goods for others between EU countries. It is issued on top of the Czech licence, and one of the conditions is a registered transport manager. Transport inside another EU country (cabotage) is only allowed to a limited extent. We help you meet all conditions and arrange the Community licence for you.
The Czech road haulage licence (koncese) allows domestic transport of goods over 3.5 t in the Czech Republic. The Community licence builds on it and allows international transport within the EU. Both require a registered transport manager with proven professional competence, which is exactly what we provide.
